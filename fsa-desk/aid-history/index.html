Aid History — My Aid

Everything a borrower can see about their own loans and grants

Official resources

  • What is “My Aid”?

Aid history lives behind the borrower's own login · General help: 1-800-433-3243

“Where do I see everything I've ever gotten?”

In My Aid — part of your StudentAid.gov account, reached by logging in. It holds the detailed information about your loan and grant aid. (There is no public, logged-out view of anyone's aid — it lives behind the borrower's own account, by design.)

““My Aid” is part of your account on StudentAid.gov”— the site’s own words, source page
Open the source on StudentAid.gov ↗

“What exactly can I see in My Aid?”

Loan and grant aid detail, plus five histories the article names: subsidized usage (for Direct Subsidized Loans on or after July 1, 2013 and before July 1, 2021), subsidy history (overpayment history), loan status history, enrollment history, and payment history.

“You can view detailed information about your loan and grant aid”— the site’s own words, source page

“How far back does My Aid go? Can my parent see my history?”

THE SITE DOESN’T SAY — gap on the source page

THE ARTICLE DOESN'T SAY: how far back the history reaches, whether discharged, defaulted, or paid-in-full loans appear, whether a parent can view a dependent student's history, or how quickly new disbursements show up.
