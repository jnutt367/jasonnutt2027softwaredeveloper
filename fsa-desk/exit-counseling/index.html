Exit Counseling

Required when a borrower leaves school or drops below half-time

Official resources

  • Exit Counseling — start or demo

Help: your school's financial aid office · General: 1-800-433-3243

“How do I complete exit counseling?”

★ Log in at StudentAid.gov/exit-counseling (“Log In To Start”) with a verified StudentAid.gov account — a “View Demo” preview sits on the same page. It takes about 30 minutes and must be finished IN ONE SESSION: you cannot save and return to an incomplete session, and sessions time out after 15 minutes of inactivity. Have ready: your verified account, your school name(s), and updated contact information.

“Exit counseling takes about 30 minutes to complete”— the site’s own words, source page

“Who has to do exit counseling, and when?”

Student borrowers: you must complete it when you graduate, leave school, or drop below half-time enrollment — including withdrawing, and including leaving for any reason. You must complete it again whenever you leave a school, even if you plan to restart another program later.

“You must complete exit counseling when you leave school or drop below half-time enrollment”— the site’s own words, source page
Open the source on StudentAid.gov ↗

“I'm a parent PLUS borrower — do I have to do it?”

No. Parent PLUS Loan borrowers are not required to complete exit counseling.

“Parent PLUS Loan borrowers are not required to complete exit counseling”— the site’s own words, source page
Open the source on StudentAid.gov ↗

“What does exit counseling actually cover?”

Its purpose, in the page's words, is to ensure you understand your student loan obligations and are prepared for repayment. You'll learn what your federal student loan payments will look like after school, and the session recommends a repayment strategy suited to your plans and goals.

“The purpose of exit counseling is to ensure you understand your student loan obligations and are prepared for repayment”— the site’s own words, source page
Open the source on StudentAid.gov ↗

“I need help with it — or my school does it differently?”

Your school may have alternate exit counseling requirements — check with your school's financial aid office, which is also the page's stated help route for completing it. General help: the Federal Student Aid Information Center at 1-800-433-3243, or live chat in your StudentAid.gov account. Contact information itself is updated via Communications Settings.

“Your school may have alternate exit counseling requirements. Check with your school's financial aid office”— the site’s own words, source page
Open the source on StudentAid.gov ↗

“What happens if I never complete it? Is there a deadline?”

THE SITE DOESN’T SAY — gap on the source page

THE SITE DOESN'T SAY — on this page. It states no deadline for completing exit counseling, no consequence (hold, penalty, or block) for never completing it, and nothing about completing it retroactively or what proof of completion a borrower receives. Don't improvise consequences at the desk; the school's financial aid office governs its own requirements.
