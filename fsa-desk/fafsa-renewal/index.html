FAFSA — Original & Renewal

There is no separate renewal form — a new FAFSA every year

Official resources

  • Will I need to fill out the FAFSA form each year?
  • FAFSA hub

Renewal questions (FSAIC): 1-800-433-3243

“Do I have to fill out the FAFSA every year?”

Yes — file a FAFSA for each award year you're a student. Eligibility for federal student aid does not carry over from one award year to the next.

“Eligibility for federal student aid does not carry over from one award year to the next”— the site’s own words, source page
Open the source on StudentAid.gov ↗

“Is there a separate renewal form?”

No. There is no “Renewing Your FAFSA Form” page and no separate renewal form on the site — renewal means filing a new FAFSA each year, through the same routes as the first time (★ online, or the paper PDF).

“You must apply for aid every year, but with the right information in hand, it’s easy.”— the site’s own words, source page
Open the source on StudentAid.gov ↗

“Will I get the same aid amount each year?”

Not necessarily — your eligibility can differ from year to year for various reasons, so each year's form matters.

“Your eligibility for federal student aid can differ from year to year for various reasons”— the site’s own words, source page
