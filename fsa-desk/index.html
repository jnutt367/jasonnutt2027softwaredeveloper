Contacts & forms

General Federal Student Aid help — the main 1-800 line (FAFSA questions, account access, finding your servicer, anything not tied to one program): 1-800-433-3243 (1-800-4-FED-AID).

The special lines ring the program teams directly: Discharge & forgiveness support (PSLF form help, TPD, TEACH): 1-888-303-7818 · Borrower Defense hotline: 1-855-279-6207 · Defaulted loans (Default Resolution Group): 1-800-621-3115. Payments, billing, and plan changes go to the borrower's loan servicer.

PSLF paper form — mail: U.S. Department of Education, P.O. Box 300010, Greenville, TX 75403 · fax: 540-212-2415.
Borrower Defense mail: U.S. Department of Education, Federal Student Aid Information Center, P.O. Box 1854, Monticello, KY 42633.

How this reference stays true

Every card is drawn from the official StudentAid.gov page named on it, verified on the date shown. When the site changes, the cards are re-checked and swapped — this address never moves. Cards marked “the site doesn’t say” are honest gaps: the source page offers no answer, so none is invented here.
