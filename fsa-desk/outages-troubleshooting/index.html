“The site won't let me log in — is it down?”

Check the clock first: StudentAid.gov has regularly scheduled maintenance on Sundays from 3 a.m. to 11 a.m. Eastern time. During that window you can view website pages, but you cannot create an account or log in. Most “the site is down” calls on a Sunday morning are this window, not an outage.

“StudentAid.gov has regularly scheduled site maintenance on Sundays from 3 a.m. until 11 a.m. Eastern time”— the site’s own words, source page

“I got an unknown error message.”

The site's own playbook, in its order: ① clear your browser history (your “cache”) · ② close all browser windows · ③ reopen your browser · ④ try again. If you continue to experience issues after that, contact the Federal Student Aid Information Center — that's the page's stated next step, and this aid's number for it is 1-800-433-3243.

“Clear your browser history (your “cache”)”— the site’s own words, source page

“Where's the outage / status page I can check?”

There isn't one — no public real-time system-status page exists on StudentAid.gov (searched for this aid, October 22, 2026). The Announcements page carries program updates, webinars, and events — not system status. For an unplanned problem, the stated channel is the Help Center and 1-800-433-3243.

“Find important updates regarding the federal student aid programs, watch previously recorded webinars, and view a list of our upcoming events”— the site’s own words, source page

“Is my browser the problem?”

Supported browsers: the latest release of Microsoft Edge, Google Chrome, Mozilla Firefox, and Apple Safari — plus a current browser with 128-bit encryption and cookies enabled; PDFs need the free Adobe Acrobat Reader. If you're on an unsupported desktop browser, the site warns your ability to view and interact with it may be significantly impaired.

“Supported browsers: The latest release for Microsoft Edge, Google Chrome, Mozilla Firefox, and Apple Safari”— the site’s own words, source page

“Where do I send a caller for self-help?”

The Help Center (header → “Get Help”): topics include Getting Started, Managing Your Account, the FAFSA Help Center, Repaying Loans, Types of Aid, and a Glossary — with “Contact Us” for call, email, or chat with a representative. The account-access article (this aid's FSA ID area) lives there too.

“We're here to help. Call, email, or chat with a representative”— the site’s own words, source page
