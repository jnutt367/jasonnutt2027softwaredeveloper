Say it this way

Short spoken lines for the hard moments of a call. Each is one to three sentences an agent can actually say.

How to use this page

These lines are drafted for Jason’s edit, in the voice of an agent whose strength is patience and clarity. They are a starting voice, not a script — make them your own before you say them.

Every fact inside a line is the cards’ — the line names the card that carries it, and the card names its source page on StudentAid.gov. Where a line carries no fact, it says so. Nothing here is a procedure, and nothing here names a system; it is only words an agent can say.

Drafted October 28, 2026 · facts verified as the named cards state.

A denial isn’t always the last word — in many cases you can ask for the decision to be looked at again, especially if there’s new information. A death call

“First, I’m very sorry for your loss. Please don’t carry these loans as one of your worries: federal student loans are discharged when the borrower passes, and no one in the family owes them. When you’re ready — only when you’re ready — the servicer will need a copy of the death certificate; there’s no form to fill out.”Say it this way — drafted; make it your own
